Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: May 2026

The short version

Some links on Calm Veritas are affiliate links. This means if you click a link and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission — at no additional cost to you. We only link to products we believe in based on the evidence we write about.

What this means in practice

Calm Veritas participates in affiliate programs including the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

We may also partner directly with supplement brands, app developers, and wellness companies whose products align with what we write about. Any such partnership will be disclosed clearly in the relevant article or page.

Our editorial standard

Affiliate relationships never influence our editorial content. We do not write about products in order to earn commissions — we write about topics based on the evidence, and may link to relevant products where they genuinely help the reader.

If we receive a product for free, or are paid to write about something, that will be explicitly stated at the top of the article. We do not publish sponsored posts that are written or directed by the sponsor.

FTC compliance

In accordance with the FTC's guidelines on endorsements and testimonials, all material connections between Calm Veritas and any advertiser, sponsor, or affiliate partner are disclosed on this page and, where relevant, within individual articles.
